Newton's Laws of Motion

Chapter 1: Classical Mechanics
Section 1.1: Force and Acceleration
Force is defined as an interaction that changes the motion of an object. Newton's Second Law states that F = ma, where F is net force, m is mass, and a is acceleration.

Section 1.2: Inertia
Newton's First Law states that an object remains at rest or in uniform motion unless acted upon by a net external force.
